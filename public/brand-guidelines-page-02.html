02 Background Rules
Dark / Navy
Use gold-transparent as default. Switch to white-transparent over complex imagery where gold lacks contrast.
Light / White
Use navy-transparent as default. Switch to black-transparent for mono contexts — single-ink print, emboss, foil.
Emerald
Use gold-on-emerald (self-contained). white-transparent is an acceptable alternative when the gold mark lacks clear separation.

Prohibited — Never place the gold logo on white, light gray, or any light-toned background. Gold at #D4AF37 fails minimum contrast thresholds on light surfaces and violates the brand's core color rule.

03 Clear Space
Minimum Exclusion Zone
A protected clear space must surround the logo on all four sides at all times. No other graphic element, text, border, or image may enter this zone.
Clear space = X ÷ 2 per side
Defining X
X equals the full height of the ASCENDRA mark. As the logo scales up or down, the exclusion zone scales proportionally — the ratio is always fixed at half the mark height per side.
Constrained Contexts
When context forces the logo into a tight space — app icons, favicons, embossed badges — the mark may be used as a standalone symbol without a wordmark. Clear space rules still apply to the symbol's bounding box.

04 Minimum Size
32 px
Digital minimum · screen, UI, web, mobile
At this size the mark remains legible at 1× and 2× pixel density. Below 32 px, geometric detail is lost — use the dedicated favicon asset instead.
15 mm
Print minimum · documents, collateral, packaging
At 15 mm (≈ 57 px at 96 dpi) the mark holds all geometric detail at 300 dpi print resolution. Below this threshold, fine lines close up in offset reproduction.
05 Misuse
✕
Do not distort
Never stretch, compress, or alter the mark's aspect ratio. Scale uniformly only — width and height must change at the same rate.
✕
Do not recolor
Only the approved color variants may be used. Do not apply custom tints, gradients, or filters to the mark.
✕
Do not use on busy backgrounds
The mark requires a clean, solid-color field. Patterns, textures, and complex imagery break its geometric legibility.
✕
Do not rotate
The mark must always appear upright. No rotation, reflection, or flip is permitted in any orientation or context.
