### REVISED

**5.8A Selected Work**

**Purpose:** Bridge the argument (problem → solution → outcomes) to real evidence. Prevents the visitor from reaching the Contact CTA without having seen proof that ASCENDRA delivers real projects. Three items surface the breadth of work without requiring the visitor to navigate to /work.

```
[Eyebrow]            Selected Work
[H2]                 What we have built.

[Three preview items — card treatment, left to right]

  PharmacyOS
  ──────────────────────
  [Tag]    Flagship Product
  [Desc]   A complete operational management system for pharmacies —
           inventory, prescriptions, suppliers, reporting.
  [Link]   View Project →     → /work

  Taghreed Academy
  ──────────────────────
  [Tag]    Case Study · Education
  [Desc]   [One-line description of challenge or outcome]
  [Link]   View Case Study →  → /work

  WIN Solutions
  ──────────────────────
  [Tag]    Digital Presence
  [Desc]   [One-line description of what was built]
  [Link]   View Project →     → /work

[Section CTA]
  [View All Work →]           → /work
```

**Placement rule:** Immediately after Business Outcomes. Before Conviction Statement. The sequence reads: here is the problem (Bridge) → here is what we do (Digital + Consulting) → here is why we exist (Why ASCENDRA) → here is how we work (Method) → here is what changes (Outcomes) → **here is proof we do it (Selected Work)** → here is the conviction (Conviction Statement) → here is how to start (Contact CTA).

**Card distinctions:**
- PharmacyOS card carries the tag "Flagship Product" — not "Case Study" or "Project." This distinction is visible from Home.
- Taghreed Academy card carries "Case Study" tag.
- WIN Solutions card carries "Digital Presence" tag.
- All three link to /work. None link to individual project detail pages (those do not exist in this version).

---

### REASON

Home had no proof layer. A visitor could read the full homepage — problem, pillars, method, outcomes — and reach the Contact CTA without ever seeing a single real project. The "See What We Build" CTA in the hero points to /work, but it competes with "Let's Talk" for the visitor's early attention and many visitors will not click it before scrolling.

The Selected Work section places evidence inside the homepage scroll path — not behind a navigation link. It also performs a secondary function: it surfaces the three-tier work structure (Flagship Product, Case Study, Digital Presence) on Home, orienting the visitor before they reach /work.

PharmacyOS is listed first and tagged as "Flagship Product" — establishing its elevated status at the point of first exposure on Home.

---

### REASON

The original bio was credential-listing in the style of a LinkedIn summary: "seven years of experience" + three domain labels. It communicated competence but not conviction. It did not explain why ASCENDRA exists or why Hossam is the person to build it.

The revised version replaces credentials with narrative. It traces a specific arc: pharmacist → observed the systems gap → led operations and sales → built PharmacyOS → founded ASCENDRA. Each step makes the next one credible. The founding of ASCENDRA is presented as an inevitability, not an aspiration.

The secondary role line ("Pharmacist · Operations Leader · Business Systems Builder") replaces the generic years-of-experience opener and surfaces the pharmacist origin immediately — before the bio begins. Visitors who skim headers get the story without reading the full bio.

PharmacyOS is named explicitly in the Founder section because the Founder is its creator. This creates a reciprocal proof loop: PharmacyOS validates Hossam's capability, and Hossam's story validates PharmacyOS as a genuine systems product rather than a client deliverable.

**Visual hierarchy note:**
The PharmacyOS section occupies more vertical space and receives more content depth than any other item on the Work page. The visual treatment — full-width header, product screenshots, multi-block content structure — must be visually distinct from the card-based treatment of Case Studies and Digital Presence Projects below it.

The tier label changes from "FEATURED PRODUCT" to "FLAGSHIP PRODUCT" to signal that this item is categorically different from the work below it — not just ranked first, but of a different nature entirely.

---

### REASON

In v1.0, PharmacyOS received the same structural treatment as the Case Studies below it — a single card block with a description, pillar flags, and a status line. The label "FEATURED PRODUCT" placed it above the case studies, but the content depth was not proportionally greater.

PharmacyOS is ASCENDRA's only proprietary product. It is the primary proof that ASCENDRA builds real, functional systems — not just client deliverables. It requires a treatment that communicates:

1. This is a product ASCENDRA owns, not a project it was hired to do.
2. The problem it solves is real and documented.
3. The founder built it from direct professional experience.
4. Both ASCENDRA pillars (Digital and Consulting) were applied.

The "FLAGSHIP PRODUCT" label replaces "FEATURED PRODUCT" because "featured" implies editorial ranking within a portfolio. "Flagship" communicates that this product represents ASCENDRA's full capability and conviction — it is the reference standard, not the top-ranked item in a list.

The explicit status note ("PharmacyOS is not a client project") is intentional. Visitors reading a Work page will assume everything on it was commissioned by a client. This assumption must be corrected for PharmacyOS — because the distinction is what makes it significant.

This sequence reinforces the ASCENDRA Method (Assess → Design → Build) within the service list itself.

The description ends with "before a single line of code is written" — this phrase explicitly distinguishes Business Systems Design from the implementation services that follow it. It also reinforces the Consulting foundation beneath every Digital engagement.
