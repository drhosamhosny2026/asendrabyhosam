**Version:** v1.1

A neutral, page-by-page review of the current website against the new positioning: **Business Systems Company** (not a digital agency, not two separate pillars).

---

## The Core Finding

The current website is architected around **two pillars: ASCENDRA Digital + ASCENDRA Consulting.** This framing appears everywhere — the hero ("Two pillars. One system."), the bridge section, the consulting section, the insight cards, and the method ("across every Digital and Consulting engagement").

This two-pillar structure **is** the agency/consultancy duality. A business systems company sells **one integrated offering** — a system — where digital and operational work are *components*, not two services a client chooses between.

> **The realignment is not renaming services. It is collapsing "two pillars" into "one system."** This single decision drives the entire reconstruction.

**The seed already exists.** The "Why ASCENDRA" section already speaks in systems language ("the solution is rarely more effort — it is better systems"). The correct message is already inside the site, buried under the two-pillar structure. We are surfacing it, not inventing it.

## What the Audit Tells the Next Phase

The audit confirms the realignment is **repackaging, not renaming**. The central decision it surfaces for the Service Architecture phase:

> **The "Digital / Consulting" two pillars must be removed — not just the section, but the idea — from the entire site.** The client should no longer ask *"do I need Digital or Consulting?"* but *"which system does my business need now?"*
