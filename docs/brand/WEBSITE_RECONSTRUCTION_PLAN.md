---

## PHASE 1

### Home — new structure

```
Hero
  ↓
Core Systems            (teaser — the 4 systems, one line each, link to Solutions)
  ↓
Why Better Systems Matter   (the "Why ASCENDRA" insight — already on-message)
  ↓
Outcomes                (what changes after ASCENDRA)
  ↓
Selected Systems & Work (proof — real projects framed as systems; PharmacyOS flagship)
  ↓
CTA
```

**Rules:**
- **Remove the two-pillar model entirely** (ASCENDRA Digital / ASCENDRA Consulting,
  the bridge, the dual cards, "Two pillars. One system."). The idea, not just the section.
- **Core Systems is a teaser, not a catalog.** One line per system + a link to Solutions.
  The full breakdown lives on Solutions — do not duplicate it on Home.
- **Selected Systems & Work is proof, not a second systems list.** Real projects,
  framed as business systems, focused on outcomes.
- Remove the flat `capabilities` (13-item) list — it is an agency menu and is redundant.
- Hero already corrected (headline, tagline). Rewrite `subtext` / `keyMessage` to
  systems language (no service list, no "two pillars").

### Solutions — new structure (formerly Services)

```
Introduction            (one system company, not a service list)
  ↓
Business Analysis & Assessment   (the Entry Point — diagnosis first)
  ↓
Foundation System
  ↓
Operations System
  ↓
Performance & Intelligence System
  ↓
Custom Business Systems
  ↓
CTA
```

**Rules:**
- This page changes the company from "a list of services" to "a system company."
  It is the heart of the reconstruction.
- Each system follows the fixed pattern: **Problem → System → Capabilities → Outcome.**
  The Problem line comes first — it makes the client say "that's me."
- The four systems read as a **journey** (Foundation → Operations → Performance →
  Transformation), not a menu.
- Remove the `#digital` / `#consulting` two-pillar sections and the parallel,
  divergently-worded service groups. One source of truth for each capability.
